## Imagery

Product, category, collection and banner imagery is photography from Unsplash, downloaded and committed under `public/images/photos/` (never hotlinked). Every file is listed in the table below with its photo id, photographer and page. All are under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use commercially, no permission needed, attribution appreciated. Frames were chosen for plain, unbranded garments and shoes; anything with a visible logo or wordmark was rejected during selection, and nothing here is presented as another brand's product.

How the files are produced (`scripts/photos/`):

- `search.mjs` collects candidates per subject from Unsplash search, skips Unsplash+ (paid) results and anything whose description names a brand, and builds numbered contact sheets for a human pass.
- `review.mjs` builds close-up sheets of the picks so small marks show before anything is downloaded.
- `manifest.json` maps every product colour, category, collection and banner to photo ids; `download.mjs` fetches the originals, crops to 3:4 (1200 × 1600) or 16:9 / 4:5 for banners with an attention-weighted crop, encodes WebP at quality 80, generates a 16 px inline blur placeholder, and writes `prisma/seed/data/photos.ts` plus the table below.

Every seeded product has at least two frames per colour, so the listing hover swap always has a second image. Where a colourway has no plausible frame the manifest reuses another colour's photos and says so in its `note`; those notes are the honest record of what is a stand-in (for example, Hush Ridge in Olive reuses the Slate frames, and several teal or lilac garments stand in for Storm and Dusk).

Alt text describes the garment and colour ("Seam Hood in Bone, detail"), never a brand or a person.

Generated assets that remain: `prisma/seed/lib/placeholders.ts` still produces an SVG tile or product placeholder for any product or category that has no photograph in the manifest, and a solid-colour blur placeholder as the fallback when a photo has no inline blur. `next.config.ts` allows SVG through `next/image` with a content security policy that blocks scripts inside images, for that fallback only.

Repository footprint: `public/images/photos/` is about 17 MB for 146 files (largest 570 KB).
